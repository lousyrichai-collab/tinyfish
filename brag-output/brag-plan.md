# brag-plan — Ray-Ban Meta (Gen 2) Hindi reel

**Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 30fps, 21s
**Tone:** Indian tech-YouTuber reel. High-energy Hinglish, bold yellow/red captions on black, punch-zoom cuts, spec cards, a "kya aapko lena chahiye?" verdict. A generic style, not modelled on any one creator: no channel names, faces or catchphrases borrowed from a real person.

## What it is
AI glasses in Ray-Ban frames with a camera, open-ear speakers and Meta AI you talk to. The India angle: **they now understand Hindi.**

## Source facts (all from meta.com/in and Meta's India newsroom, fetched 2026-10-18)
- Full Hindi support: ask questions, take photos and videos, answer calls and texts, control media, all in Hindi (about.fb.com, India Newsroom)
- Live Cricket Updates: "Hey Meta, follow…" a team, then get AI-narrated wicket alerts and score updates (same post)
- Ray-Ban Meta (Gen 2) Wayfarer: wide-angle 12 MP camera, up to 3K video at 30fps, up to 8 hours of battery plus 48 more from the case, 5-mic array, open-ear speakers (meta.com/in compare page)
- Price: starting at ₹31,920, down from ₹39,900 (meta.com/in compare page; a sale price that can change)

Left out on purpose: UPI payments (still "testing" / "soon"), the celebrity voice (a real person's likeness), and any score numbers or other invented claims.

## Angle
"दोस्तों, ये चश्मा हिंदी समझता है!" The surprise of talking to your glasses in Hindi, backed by cricket alerts and hard specs, ending on an honest verdict.

## Visual identity
Black (#0B0B0F) background. Hindi-YouTuber yellow (#FFD400) and red (#E8202A) for captions and bars, white body text. Baloo 2 ExtraBold for headlines, Mukta for body text. The glasses are an SVG Wayfarer drawn by hand, because Meta's image CDN is blocked from this machine.
